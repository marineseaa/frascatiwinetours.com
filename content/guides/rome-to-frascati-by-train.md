Take a regional FL4 train from Roma Termini to Frascati. [Trenitalia](https://www.trenitalia.com/en/connections/castelli-romani-line.html) gives the ride as about 30 minutes, and a regional ticket costs a couple of euros each way. From the station, a flight of steps brings you up to Frascati's main square. Going it alone is easy if you want the town, the gardens and a fraschetta lunch. Book a tour if you want a proper winery visit, because most cellars take visitors by appointment only and many need a taxi from the station, or if you're travelling on a Saturday, when several tours change their meeting point.

> **Rome's original wine train.** The Rome–Frascati line opened in 1856 as the first railway in the Papal States. It's an ordinary commuter train, not a dining train, but it still drops you at the foot of Rome's wine country.

## How do you get from Rome to Frascati by train?

1. **Go to Roma Termini.** Frascati trains leave from the main station. Platforms vary, so check the departure board.
2. **Check the destination says FRASCATI.** The FL4 line splits into three branches at Ciampino. Trains to Albano Laziale or Velletri won't take you to Frascati.
3. **Buy your ticket before you board** (see below).
4. **Ride about 30 minutes**, via Capannelle and Ciampino. Frascati is the end of the line, so you can't miss your stop.
5. **Climb the steps** from the station to Piazza Marconi, about 5 minutes on foot.

## What are the train times from Rome to Frascati?

We don't print a fixed timetable, because the departures change with the day of the week, with engineering works and with each new timetable. The next change is on 13 December 2026. Search Roma Termini to Frascati in the Trenitalia app or on [trenitalia.com](https://www.trenitalia.com/) for your exact date.

### Roma Termini → Frascati

Departures are not evenly spaced through the day, and some run on weekdays only. Look at the late-morning trains before you fix a lunch or tasting time, and pick a train that arrives at least 15 minutes before any meeting time.

### Frascati → Roma Termini

Some trains from Frascati run only as far as Ciampino, where you change for Rome. Check the destination on the board and in the app.

### What time is the last train from Frascati to Rome?

It depends on the day: weekends and public holidays usually have fewer evening trains than weekdays. Look it up in the Trenitalia app for your date before you settle in for dinner, and set an alarm.

### The mid-morning gap

On many days the trains thin out in the late morning. If you want to arrive for a morning in town, aim for an early train; if you're coming for lunch, a late-morning train is enough.

## Why is Saturday different?

The Saturday timetable differs from the weekday one, and several Saturday wine tours meet at Tor Vergata station instead of Frascati. Tor Vergata is on a different line (towards Cassino) and sits on the plain below Frascati, about a 25-minute uphill walk from town, so it's only useful if your tour or a taxi collects you there.

## How do you buy and validate a train ticket to Frascati?

### Which ticket?

A Trenitalia regional ticket from Roma Termini to Frascati. It's a standard Lazio regional fare of a couple of euros. Buy two tickets for a day trip, one each way.

> **Rome city tickets don't work.** The BIT ticket and Rome's 24/48/72-hour passes don't cover the trip, because Frascati is outside the city fare zone.

### Where to buy

- **Trenitalia app or website** (easiest)
- **Ticket machines** at Termini
- **Ticket offices** and authorised outlets (*tabaccherie*)

### Can I buy on the train?

Don't plan on it. Buying on board, where it's allowed at all, costs a surcharge, and boarding without a ticket can mean a fine. It's simpler to buy in advance.

### Paper tickets

- **Validate before boarding** in the machines on the platform.
- A validated regional ticket is good for one journey within a few hours, so you need a separate return ticket.
- Forgot to validate? Find the conductor as soon as you board.

### Digital tickets (app or website)

- Read the conditions on the ticket: some are tied to a date or a named train, and some need a check-in in the app before you board.
- **Tip:** buy your return ticket on the platform once you know which train you'll catch.

### Contactless?

Trenitalia offers contactless Tap&Tap regional tickets on some routes. Check in the app whether it covers Frascati before you rely on it; otherwise buy a ticket.

### Fines

Travelling without a valid ticket means a fine on top of the fare, so it's not worth the risk.

## Strikes and engineering works

Italian rail strikes are announced in advance, and some regional trains are guaranteed at commuter hours (weekdays 06:00–09:00 and 18:00–21:00). Before you travel, check the strikes and service notices on [Trenitalia's Infomobilità page](https://www.trenitalia.com/it/informazioni/Infomobilita/notizie-infomobilita.html) and Italy's Ministry of Transport strike calendar.

The Frascati line has also had engineering closures, including in summer, when replacement buses can run to Anagnina (Metro A) instead of Termini. RFI posts planned works on its [Infomobilità page](https://www.rfi.it/it/news-e-media/infomobilita.html).

**How to check:**
- Trenitalia's Infomobilità and planned-works notices
- The Trenitalia app
- Italy's Ministry of Transport strike calendar

## How do you get from Frascati station to the town centre?

Climb the stone staircase from the station forecourt up to Piazza Marconi, the town's main square. It's about 250–300 metres and around 5 minutes. At the top you'll find the Villa Aldobrandini gates, the tourist information point, the Scuderie Aldobrandini museum and cafés.

**Accessibility:** the stairs to the square are the main obstacle, so wheelchair users and travellers with heavy luggage should plan a taxi or a private transfer. Ask RFI's assistance service before you travel whether help is available at Frascati on your date.

## Can you visit Frascati wineries without a tour?

Yes, but plan it like a restaurant booking, because you can't just walk in. Most estates are out in the countryside, not in the old town, so you'll need a taxi from the station and an appointment.

## A DIY Frascati wine day: three plans

### Half day (weekday)

- A mid-morning train from Termini, arriving before about 10:30
- Climb to Piazza Marconi and ask at the tourist office whether the Villa Aldobrandini gardens are open
- Wander the old town; buy porchetta at Piazza del Mercato
- **Fraschetta lunch** with a jug of house wine
- An afternoon train back

### Full day with a winery (weekday)

- An early train from Termini
- The old town and, if open, Villa Aldobrandini's gardens in the morning
- **Pre-booked cellar visit and tasting**, with a taxi there and back
- Afternoon in town; a *pupazza* biscuit and a coffee
- **Fraschetta dinner** in a tufa cellar
- One of the evening trains back; check the last one for your date

### Sunday

- A morning train from Termini
- Villa Torlonia park, the cathedral and the Scuderie museum (open Tuesday to Sunday)
- Long Sunday lunch: book ahead, as it's the busiest meal of the week
- Check the Sunday last train before lunch, as Sunday evenings have fewer trains

### Add Castel Gandolfo

There's no direct train between Frascati and Castel Gandolfo; they're on different branches. A Cotral bus runs from Frascati via Marino to Castel Gandolfo in roughly half an hour, and you can then take an FL4 train back to Termini from the Albano branch. Check Cotral times before you go.

## Is this the "wine train"?

Not the famous one. If you're searching for the Napa Valley Wine Train, the gourmet dining train in California, that's a different experience entirely.

But the Rome–Frascati railway has a fair claim to being Rome's original wine train:

- **1856:** the line opens as the first railway in the Papal States.
- **The early years:** the first terminus stood about 3 km short of Frascati, below the town, and the town's wine still went to Rome by cart.
- **1884:** the line was extended to today's station, right below the main square.
- **1943:** the station was destroyed in the bombing of 8 September and rebuilt after the war.
- **Today:** a commuter train, a cheap ticket, and about 30 minutes to the vineyards.

## FAQ

### How long is the train from Rome to Frascati?

About 30 minutes from Roma Termini on a regional FL4 train, via Capannelle and Ciampino.

### How often do trains run from Rome to Frascati?

It varies by day, with fewer trains on Sundays and public holidays and gaps in the late morning. Check the Trenitalia app for your date.

### How much is the train ticket from Rome to Frascati?

A standard Lazio regional fare of a couple of euros each way. Buy a separate ticket for each direction.

### Can I use a Rome metro (BIT) ticket to get to Frascati?

No. Rome's BIT ticket and city passes don't cover Frascati, which is outside the city fare zone. You need a Trenitalia regional ticket.

### Can I buy a ticket on the train to Frascati?

Don't count on it. Buying on board, where allowed, costs a surcharge, and boarding without a ticket can mean a fine. Buy before boarding via the app, a machine or the ticket office.

### Can I use contactless to pay for the train to Frascati?

Trenitalia's contactless Tap&Tap tickets cover some regional routes. Check in the app whether Frascati is included; otherwise buy a ticket.

### What time is the last train from Frascati to Rome?

It depends on the day of the week and the timetable, which changes on 13 December 2026. Check the Trenitalia app for your date.

### Which platform does the Frascati train leave from at Termini?

Platforms vary. Check the departure board at Termini for a train showing FRASCATI as its destination.

### How do I get from Frascati station to the town centre?

Climb the stone steps from the station to Piazza Marconi, about a 5-minute walk.

### Is Frascati station accessible?

Steep stairs lead up to town from the station. Travellers with limited mobility should arrange a taxi or private transfer and ask RFI about assistance before they travel.

### Can I visit Frascati wineries without a tour?

Yes, but you'll need to book ahead, sometimes several days in advance, and take a taxi from the station. Some independent visits are only in Italian. Fraschette in town are walk-in.

### Is a guided tour or DIY better for Frascati?

DIY suits weekdays, the town and fraschette. A guided tour suits Saturdays, first-timers and non-Italian speakers, and anyone who wants a real winery visit without organising taxis and appointments.

### Is there a bus from Rome to Frascati?

Yes. Take Metro A to Anagnina, then a Cotral bus to Frascati, about 25–35 minutes from Anagnina.
