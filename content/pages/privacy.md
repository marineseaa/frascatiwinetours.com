## About this website

Frascati Wine Tours is an independent information and affiliate website. This local build does not provide user accounts, a newsletter form or direct payment collection. Fonts and site photographs are hosted with the website.

## GetYourGuide services

Pages load GetYourGuide’s affiliate analytics script, and some pages include its booking widgets. These third-party services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and the page visited. Bookings and payments take place through GetYourGuide, under its own terms and [privacy statement](https://www.getyourguide.com/privacy_policy/).

## Affiliate links

Booking links may include a partner identifier and a campaign name so GetYourGuide can attribute a referral. We may receive a commission when a booking results from one of these links.

## Hosting and external websites

Once the site is hosted, the hosting provider may process standard request logs. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices. For questions about a GetYourGuide reservation or information submitted there, use [GetYourGuide support](https://www.getyourguide.com/contact/).
